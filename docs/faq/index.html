Straight answers before you try ROCO.

The current Android preview, explained without hidden prerequisites.

Is there a WHOOP alternative for Google Pixel Watch on Android?

ROCO is one option to evaluate. It uses available Google Health or Health Connect records for its own body-energy, sleep and weekly-strain estimates. It does not reproduce WHOOP scores or every WHOOP feature.

Can I get a Body Battery-like score on Pixel Watch?

ROCO has an independent body-energy model and daily timeline. It is not Garmin Body Battery and cannot be compared point-for-point. Suitable sleep, HRV history and permissions are required.

Are these official Google or Fitbit scores?

No. ROCO calculates its own local wellness estimates. It does not unlock a proprietary Google, Fitbit or WHOOP algorithm.

Is ROCO free and open source?

The current preview APK can be downloaded without paying ROCO, and the original source is available under Apache 2.0. External AI services may charge or impose quotas. Third-party components retain their licenses.

Does every Pixel Watch work identically?

No. Device, software, source apps, permissions and API eligibility affect which records arrive. No all-model certification is claimed.

Do I need Google Health or Health Connect?

You need one accessible data source with the relevant records. ROCO prefers configured Google Health and uses Health Connect as the fallback. Direct Google Health authorization is not automatically available to every account.

Does the AI coach need my health data?

AI is optional. Local scores do not need a model API. When you use configured coaching with permitted context or attachments, that data can be sent to external providers.

Why is a score missing or from an earlier day?

Missing permissions, incomplete imports, insufficient personal history, source conflicts or a stale night can prevent new calculation. A saved fallback retains its original timestamp.

Can ROCO diagnose illness or overtraining?

No. A low energy score or weekly strain above 100 is not a diagnosis. Scores should be interpreted with symptoms, training context and professional advice where needed.

Is there XP or a level system?

The current app has an animated, nameable robot and journal check-in streaks. A broader XP/level system is not promised.
