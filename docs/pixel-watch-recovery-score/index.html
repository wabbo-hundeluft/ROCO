YOUR WATCH. YOUR CONTEXT.

Get more context from your Pixel Watch data.

A Pixel Watch does not need to provide a ready-made ROCO score. ROCO reads compatible records from Google Health or Health Connect and calculates its own estimates locally. The phone app is not a Wear OS sensor app.

Start with data, not a score

  1. Sync your watch with its companion health app.
  2. In ROCO, connect an eligible Google Health account, or choose Health Connect as the fallback.
  3. Grant the relevant read permissions and confirm that the records actually exist in the selected source.
  4. Refresh ROCO and inspect the data-source and timestamp details.

What measurements help?

Sleep sessions and stages describe the night. HRV and resting heart rate are compared with personal history. Workouts, heart-rate samples and steps describe activity. Depending on the Google Health response, ROCO can also display oxygen saturation, respiratory rate and skin-temperature deviation; these extra vital signals do not automatically enter every score.

Why is my body-energy score unavailable?

The current energy model requires a suitable main sleep and usable current HRV with its personal baseline. Missing permissions, incomplete reads, unavailable history, source conflicts or an old night can prevent calculation. Resting heart rate and other missing components can make a result provisional. A stored value keeps its original date; it is not a new measurement.

What if stages are missing?

Sleep can be provisional when stage information is missing or partial. Available components are reweighted; data are not invented. A short night still limits the combined sleep result.

Do I need paid AI?

No AI provider is needed for local scores. AI explanations require a configured provider key; free quotas, model access and charges are controlled by that provider. You can use ROCO without sending health context to an AI service.
