Check the data path before you install.

ROCO runs on your Android phone, not directly on your watch. A Pixel Watch can supply useful records through its health apps, but a watch model alone does not guarantee that every required field reaches ROCO.

Data used by ROCO

The core score path uses sleep sessions/stages, RMSSD HRV, resting heart rate, workout sessions, heart-rate samples and steps. Extra daily oxygen saturation, respiratory rate and sleep-temperature deviation are implemented in the Google connector when returned. Do not assume equivalent extras on every connector.

Known limitations

  • The current APK is a development-signed preview, not a store release.
  • Historical imports can be incomplete or restricted; reconstructed dates are estimates using available records.
  • Source conflicts, timezone boundaries and incomplete nights can leave results unavailable or provisional.
  • Data from the selected connector are kept consistent; Google Health and Health Connect are not pooled indiscriminately.
  • Automated checks do not establish clinical validity or universal device compatibility.

Troubleshooting

  1. Verify the selected connector and last successful import.
  2. Inspect actual records and their dates in that source.
  3. Review read permissions and any required history access.
  4. For Google authorization failures, compare the app's displayed package/SHA-1 with your Android OAuth client and check account eligibility.
  5. Refresh manually. If it still fails, file a redacted compatibility report with app/OS version, connector, missing data types and steps.

What has actually been checked?

The 2.0.20 preview build and 46 targeted checks passed during development. A clean staged-source build and four language tests also passed; the later English gallery uses six render tests plus four language tests. These are scoped checks, not a claim that the entire suite or every live provider has passed. Earlier Pixel 8 testing does not certify all Pixel Watch models.
