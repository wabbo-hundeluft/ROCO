What is ROCO?

ROCO is an Android health companion for people who want to understand recovery-related trends using the watch they already have. It combines local score calculations, visual history, a habit journal and training plans. AI is optional and uses your own provider API keys.

Three ways to make sense of your week.

01 / ENERGY

Your day, as a battery.

See an estimated morning energy reserve and how wake time, workouts and steps use it. It is a model, not a stress sensor.

02 / SLEEP

More than hours in bed.

Explore stages, consistency, HRV and resting heart rate when available, with transparent missing-data states.

03 / STRAIN

A week that leaves room for rest.

100 points means your personal weekly target. Your target adapts to recorded history; extra points show extra load.
