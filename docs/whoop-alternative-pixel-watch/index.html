YOUR WATCH. YOUR CONTEXT.

WHOOP-like insights. Your existing Pixel Watch.

ROCO is an option to evaluate if you want recovery-related insights from an existing Pixel Watch. It calculates its own body-energy, sleep and weekly-strain estimates from available data. It does not reproduce WHOOP's proprietary scores or replace every WHOOP feature.

Can I compare the numbers directly?

No. ROCO's 70 energy points are not WHOOP 70% Recovery. Its weekly strain is not WHOOP Day Strain. Different windows, inputs and models mean numbers can disagree even on the same day.

When should I try ROCO?

If you already have compatible records and want a transparent Android preview, local scoring, a habit journal and optional AI explanations. First check compatibility and known limitations. It is not a substitute for medical advice or a promise of full WHOOP feature parity.
